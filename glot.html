02 — What Glot does

Everything your team forgot to do

Glot doesn't wait to be asked. It listens in the background and steps in at exactly the right moment.

01 🧠
Thread Intelligence
Reads every message in a channel and builds a living summary. Ask Glot "what did I miss?" and get a clean TL;DR in seconds.
02 ⚡
Instant Routing
Glot identifies who owns what and pings them directly. No more messages disappearing into the void.
03 🔗
Cross-channel Context
Context doesn't get lost between channels. Glot carries decisions, assets, and blockers wherever they need to go.
04 📋
Decision Extraction
Pulls every decision from a thread and logs them cleanly. Your team always knows what was agreed and when.
05 🎯
Action Tracking
Spots action items in conversation and follows up automatically. Nothing falls through the cracks.
06 🌱
Gets Smarter Daily
Learns your team's language, shortcuts, and patterns. The longer Glot's around, the more useful it becomes.
"

Bring Glot into Slack and intelligence stops being a destination you visit — it becomes a teammate you talk to. Glot listens in the channels you already live in, turns scattered threads into clear next steps, and hands context off between people and tools without a single tab-switch.
